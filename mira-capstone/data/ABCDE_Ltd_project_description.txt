ABCDE Ltd. - Customer Self-Service Portal Modernization
Project description, version 1.2

BACKGROUND
ABCDE Ltd. is a mid-sized telecom services provider with about 1.4 million customers. Customers manage their accounts through a legacy web portal built in 2014 and through the call centre. Billing and plan-change calls to the call centre are high, and the legacy portal does not work well on mobile devices.

OBJECTIVE
Replace the legacy portal with a modern, mobile-friendly Customer Self-Service Portal. Target: reduce billing-related call-centre contacts by 25% within 6 months of go-live.

SCOPE (IN)
- Single sign-on (SSO) login
- Account profile management
- Billing history and invoice download
- Online bill payment through a third-party payment gateway
- Refunds for failed payments
- SMS and email notifications
- Migration of existing customer account data

SCOPE (OUT)
- Redesign of the ABCDE mobile app
- Changes to the billing engine
- New product catalogue features

TIMELINE
